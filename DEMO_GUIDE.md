# Demo Capture Guide: How to Show Off Your Work 📸

For a strong GitHub portfolio, capture these specific moments:

1. **The "Pulse" (GIF)**: 
   - Record a screen-GIF of you clicking "New Signal".
   - Show the loading spinner, and the smooth "pop-in" animation of the new post.
   - **Tip**: Shows your understanding of "Async UI" and "Real-time feeds".

2. **The "Brain" (Screenshot)**:
   - Capture a post card specifically showing the **"AI INSIGHT"** italic section.
   - This proves you aren't just using regular expressions, but a deep-learning model.

3. **The "Distribution" (Screenshot)**:
   - Show the Pie Chart and Bar Chart side-by-side. 
   - **Tip**: Great for showing your Data Visualization skills.

4. **The "Responsive" (Screenshot)**:
   - Take a screenshot of the dashboard on your phone or in mobile view.
   - Shows you are a developer who cares about UX.

5. **The "Repository" (Screenshot)**:
   - Once uploaded, screenshot your GitHub repo showing the folder structure and the beautiful README.
